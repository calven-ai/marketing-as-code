# Messaging framework template

Open this in procedure steps 2 and 5. Keep these sections in this order, with these headings and
table columns. Replace every `_italic guidance_` with a message, a labelled inference or a gap
marker. Never leave guidance in place, never cite it, and never add an "evidence" column.

## Where each section comes from

| Messaging section | Derived from the positioning |
| --- | --- |
| Core Narrative & One-Liner | Positioning Statement, reworded for buyers |
| Value Pillars | Unique Attributes plus Value Themes |
| Value Propositions by Persona | Value Themes "who cares most" crossed with the personas |
| Objection Handling | Competitive Alternatives |
| Proof cells, in every section | Proof Points |
| Messaging Matrix, Variations, Boilerplate | Written here from the pillars; segments from the ICP |
| Words to Use and Avoid | Market Category, Unique Attributes, buyer language from sources |

For a company with several products, the company-level framework has: Corporate Narrative &
One-Liner, Portfolio Value Pillars (from the company-level value themes), Why the Whole, Not the
Parts (the better-together case in customer language), Portfolio Objection Handling,
Boilerplate, Naming & Nomenclature (the product names and how to use them). Each product then gets
the full framework below, minus Boilerplate.

```markdown
# Messaging: <Product>

## Core Narrative & One-Liner

- **Core message:** _the one sentence everything ladders up to: the benefit and who it is for._
- **One-liner:** _what the product does and who relies on it._
- **Elevator pitch (30s):** _the problem the buyer feels, why the status quo fails, how the product solves it, closing on a quantified outcome._
- **Tagline variants:** _two or three short phrasings of the core message._

## Value Pillars

| Pillar | Headline | Supporting message | Proof point |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **_pillar name_** | _the headline claim_ | _the argument that makes it land_ | _quantified proof or customer evidence, cited_ |

## Value Propositions by Persona

| Persona | Leading value prop | Lead pillar |
| --- | --- | --- |
| _persona or buying role_ | _one line framed for what this persona cares about_ | _the pillar that leads for them_ |

## Messaging Matrix (Persona × Funnel Stage)

| Persona | Awareness (problem) | Consideration (evaluation) | Decision (justify) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| _primary buying role_ | _the problem framing that earns attention_ | _the message that frames the comparison_ | _the proof or outcome that justifies the purchase_ |

## Objection Handling

| Objection | Reframe + proof |
| --- | --- |
| _the objection in the buyer's words_ | _the reframe, backed by proof or customer evidence_ |

## Vertical & Campaign Variations

| Variation | Angle | Lead message |
| --- | --- | --- |
| _a priority vertical_ | _the angle specific to this vertical: its regulations, buyers, workflows or pains_ | _the lead message tuned for it_ |
| _a named campaign_ | _the campaign's angle_ | _the lead message for it_ |

## Boilerplate

> _who the company is, what it does, who relies on it, the proof worth citing (recognition, customer count, marquee logos), and the outcome it delivers._

## Words to Use and Avoid

| Use | Avoid | Why | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| _the term to use_ | _the term it replaces_ | _the positioning or buyer reason_ | _citation_ |

## Sources

1. _title, URL or file name, or positioning section; date_
```

Gap markers, exactly:

- Inferred: `_Inferred from <evidence>; confirm with the team._` (once under the heading when a whole section rests on it)
- No evidence: `_Not enough data._`
- Positioning missing: `_Not enough data (positioning not yet captured)._`
- Contradiction: `_Note: <source A> says X, which conflicts with the positioning's Y._`
- The user said they do not know yet: `_Not tracked yet (per <role>, <date>)._`
