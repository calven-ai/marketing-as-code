## Why this piece

Fills messaging pillar 3, "The report they forward", which has had no
proof of its own. Written for the Head of Support at the decision stage,
and for the champion, the support team lead, who forwards it. A reader
should think "a team like mine did this in one quarter".

## The argument

Fernhill, a 180-person design-tool company, had one status page for
every customer. After moving incident updates to Beacon in February 2026,
the support team lead sent updates to affected customers only and sent a
report the morning after. Incident tickets fell 31 percent in one
quarter, and the report changed the renewal conversation.
