# Synthesis frame

Condensed from the source above; rewritten as the extraction and
confidence rules this skill applies to transcripts, surveys, NPS and
reviews.

## Three kinds of input

1. **What the team already has**: interview and sales transcripts,
   survey results (segment before reading), support tickets, win-loss
   notes, NPS verbatims. Start here; it is free and first-party.
2. **What customers say in public**: review sites, forums, community
   threads, social. Second-hand and biased toward strong opinions; good
   for language, weak for frequency.
3. **New research**: interviews and surveys, when nothing above answers
   the question (`references/interviews-and-surveys.md`).

## What to extract from every source

| Field | What to write down |
| --- | --- |
| Job to be done | functional, emotional and social: what they hired the product for |
| Pain | unprompted and emotionally loaded mentions first |
| Trigger | the event that made them look for a solution |
| Desired outcome | in the customer's exact words |
| Language | phrases, shorthand, the way they name the problem |
| Alternatives | what they considered, used before, or still use alongside |
| Objection | what nearly stopped them, or still bothers them |

Tags: `pain`, `trigger`, `outcome`, `language`, `alternative`,
`objection`, `competitor`, plus the segment.

## Analysis

1. Cluster by theme.
2. Score each theme on frequency and intensity.
3. Split by segment; a theme true of one segment is not a company truth.
4. Pick five to ten verbatims per theme.
5. Flag contradictions between what people say and what they do (the
   usage snapshot, the churn list).

## Confidence

| Label | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| high | three or more independent sources, unprompted, consistent across segments |
| medium | two sources, or prompted only, or one segment |
| low | one source; needs validation before anyone builds on it |

Minimum before a conclusion: five independent data points per segment.
Themes usually stop being new by the fifth or sixth interview.

## Bias to name in Caveats

Reviewers hold strong opinions; tickets show problems only; forums skew
technical and sceptical; NPS verbatims come from people who bothered to
type. Weight the last twelve months over older material.

## Persona changes

A persona is built from evidence, five to ten data points per segment,
never invented: profile, primary job, triggers, top pains ranked, desired
outcomes, objections, alternatives, vocabulary with sources, where they
gather. A provisional persona says which proxy it rests on and is
replaced as first-party evidence arrives. The diff goes to
`strategy/personas.md` for a person to merge.
