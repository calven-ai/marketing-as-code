# The event portfolio: when in-person is worth it, the 80/20, the costs

Condensed from the source above. Use it when the question is "should we
do events at all" or "which ones next year", before planning one.

## Is in-person justified?

Prioritise events when the ICP is enterprise or multi-stakeholder,
regulated, high-touch with heavy customisation, conservative, or on
six-month-plus cycles. One security vendor found deals above $500k almost
never closed without meeting in person. Outside those profiles, spend
digital-first.

## The concentration rule

A few events produce most event pipeline (one B2B SaaS programme: three
conferences, about 70 percent). Re-rank yearly; go deeper at the winners
(speaking, side events, more people, better placement) and cut the tail.
Bigger is often worse: 100 to 200-person regional events beat 5,000-person
shows on qualified leads per dollar because the ICP concentration is
higher.

## Three types, three risk profiles

| Type | Control | Risk | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| owned (dinners, roadshows, summits, user conferences) | maximum | maximum execution risk | a six-city roadshow out-produced one company's annual conference at a third of the cost |
| trade shows and conferences | constrained format | instant audience | 120 days of preparation, not four days on site; book about 70 percent of meeting slots beforehand; satellite breakfasts and dinners beat booth traffic |
| community (small, regular) | low capital | slow ramp | about $500 a month; never pitch; the "Saturday test": would people come without an inducement? |

Post-show tiering: hot the same day, warm within 48 hours, general to a
one-week nurture, all referencing the actual conversation.

Community payoff cited: prospects at three or more community events
closed 85 percent more often with 40 percent shorter cycles.

## Cost lines for the model

| Item | Range |
| --- | --- |
| conference ticket | $1,500 to 3,000 a person |
| 10 by 10 booth | $15,000 to 40,000 |
| flights | $300 to 1,000 a person |
| hotel | $300 to 400 a night |
| booth staff | three to four people minimum |
| private dinner, 15 to 20 people | $150 to 200 a head |
| breakfast meetup | $30 to 50 a head |
| meeting room | $500 to 1,500 a day |

A significant show needs roughly five to ten solid opportunities to
justify sending a team. Judge each event on its own; "events" is not one
channel.
