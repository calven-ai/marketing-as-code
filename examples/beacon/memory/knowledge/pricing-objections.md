# Pricing objections

Current truth, updated in place; the decisions behind it are in the
decision log.

- **"The status page is free with Northstar."** Heard in six of the last
  ten discovery calls. The answer that works: agree, then ask who writes
  the customer update and who reads the postmortem. We are not selling the
  page.
- **"Per-seat pricing punishes a big support team."** Answered since
  2026-10-01: support teams over ten seats buy a per-workspace plan
  (decision log, 2026-09-15). The ten-seat cap sales used before is
  retired; teams of ten or fewer stay per seat.
- **"Pagewise is 29 dollars a month."** Heard from small dev-tools teams,
  mostly Tier 3. True, and fine for a team that posts to one page. Ask
  whether every customer should get every update, and who forwards the
  report afterwards; Pagewise has no audience segments and no report.
- **What does not come up:** the price itself. Nobody has said Beacon is
  too expensive; they have said it is another tool.
