# Holding statements and the first hours

Condensed from the source above. A holding statement is drafted by this
skill and approved by a person; nothing here is published from the repo.

## Severity and response window

| Level | Example | First statement within | Who decides |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 low | a minor error, complaints | 24 to 48 hours | PR |
| 2 medium | a bug, an outage, an incident | 4 to 24 hours | PR and leadership |
| 3 high | a breach, misconduct, a legal matter | 1 to 4 hours | executives, legal, PR |
| 4 severe | injury, regulatory, existential | immediately | CEO, board, crisis team |

## The first four hours

Hour 1, assess: confirm facts, identify who is affected, note what is
unknown, convene the deciders. Hour 2, align: brief leadership and legal,
decide what can be said now, pick one spokesperson, draft the holding
statement, plan the employee briefing. Hour 3, act: release the statement,
answer media, start monitoring, brief customer-facing teams, log
everything. Hour 4, adjust: gather updates, revise if warranted, schedule
the next cycle.

Employees hear before or with the public; affected customers at once;
media within hours; investors and partners the same day for material
matters.

## The statement, six parts

| Part | Content |
| --- | --- |
| acknowledgment | we are aware of `<situation>` |
| facts | confirmed details only |
| impact | who is affected and how |
| response | what is already being done |
| commitment | the next update and when |
| resources | where affected people get help |

Pattern: "We are aware of `<situation>` and are investigating.
`<what is being done>`. We take this seriously and will share what we
learn by `<time>`. If you are affected, `<what to do, whom to contact>`."

## Say and do not say

Say: "We made a mistake; here is what happened, what we are fixing, and
how we prevent it." "We do not have all the facts; we will share findings
by `<time>`." A specific apology to customers with what it means for them.

Do not say: "We can neither confirm nor deny"; "an isolated incident"
before it is known to be; "we take privacy seriously" without specifics;
"our lawyers advised us not to comment"; nothing at all; anything that
blames the affected.

Spokesperson: "we", the human impact first, composed, "I do not know yet"
when true, only promises that can be kept, no speculation, no improvising.

## Timeline template

T+30 minutes internal activation; T+1 hour leadership briefed; T+2 hours
holding statement out; T+4 hours first detailed update; T+24 hours the
full statement with the root cause when known; T+48 hours remediation
progress; T+1 week the post-mortem; T+30 days the process review.

## Anti-patterns

"No comment"; deleting evidence; scapegoating; legal language that reads
as defensive; guarantees of prevention; one statement then silence;
treating journalists as adversaries; declaring it over before it is;
employees learning from the news; skipping the post-mortem.
