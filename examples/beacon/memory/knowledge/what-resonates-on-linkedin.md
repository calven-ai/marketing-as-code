# What resonates on LinkedIn

Current truth for `social-post`, updated by `social-performance`. One
launch is not a pattern yet; recheck after the webinar posts in November.

- **A person's account, not the company page.** The launch post went out
  on Ana's account (2026-09-02): 18,400 impressions, 212 reactions, 31
  comments (`content/2026-09-launch-linkedin-post/`).
- **An observation before the feature.** That post opened with what a
  support team does in the first ten minutes of an outage and named the
  feature only at the end. The comments asked about the workflow.
- **Support Team Leads comment, Heads of Support read.** Write the hook
  for the person who runs the queue.
