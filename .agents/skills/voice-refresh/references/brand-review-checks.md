# What a brand review checks, and how to grade it

Condensed from the brand review skill in the knowledge work plugins. The
`review` skill in this repo runs the voice check; this list says what the
recurring findings look like, so the refresh can turn a repeated finding
into a rule in `brand/voice.md`.

## With a guide

- Voice and tone: the attributes, consistency across the piece, fit for
  the audience.
- Terminology: preferred terms, jargon where the audience does not use
  it, product and feature names spelled the guide's way.
- Messaging: the value proposition consistent with `strategy/messaging.md`;
  the positioning reinforced, not contradicted.
- Style: grammar, punctuation, formatting, numbers and dates as the guide
  decides.

## Without a guide (the fallback checks)

Clarity (the main message is prominent, sentences are short, the flow is
logical); consistency (tone stable, terms uniform, format aligned);
professionalism (grammar, typos, claims substantiated).

## Always flagged, guide or not

Unsubstantiated superlatives; missing regulatory disclaimers; competitive
comparisons with no evidence; testimonials without attribution; possible
copyright problems (an image, a quote, a lifted paragraph).

## Severity

| Level | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| High | Contradicts the voice, a compliance risk, or undermines the core message |
| Medium | Off the guide but not damaging |
| Low | A style or preference deviation |

## Output shape of a review

A summary (strengths, priorities); a findings table (location, severity,
suggestion); rewritten sections for the top three to five issues as
before and after; a separate legal and compliance list with actions.

## Turning findings into the guide

A finding that appears in three reviews is a missing rule: add the do and
don't pair, the banned term, or the tone row to `brand/voice.md`, and the
next review cites the guide instead of repeating itself.
