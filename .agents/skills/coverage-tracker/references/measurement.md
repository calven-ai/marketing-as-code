# PR measurement: definitions, share of voice, quality scoring

Condensed from the source above.

## Four levels

| Level | Measures | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| output | activity | releases sent, pitches made |
| outtake | media response | coverage volume, reach |
| outcome | business effect | traffic, leads, sentiment |
| impact | value | revenue, brand value |

Report outtakes and outcomes; list outputs in one line; never mistake
outputs for results.

## Coverage metrics

| Metric | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| volume | articles and mentions |
| quality | tier and reach of the outlets |
| share of voice | our mentions against the category's |
| message pull-through | key messages appearing in the coverage |
| spokesperson quotes | named quotes from the team |
| sentiment | positive, neutral, negative, mixed |

## Share of voice

Share of voice = our mentions / (our mentions + the competitor set's
mentions) x 100.

Segments: overall; tier 1 only; by topic; by geography. Method: a
competitive set of 5 to 10 companies, fixed monitoring keywords, a fixed
period (monthly), relevant coverage only, trended over time.

## Quality score per piece

| Factor | Range |
| --- | --- |
| outlet tier | 1 to 5 (tier 1 outlet scores 5) |
| prominence | 1 to 3 (feature 3, mention 1) |
| message inclusion | 1 to 3 (all key messages 3) |
| spokesperson quote | 0 to 2 (named quote 2) |
| sentiment | minus 2 to plus 2 |

Sum the factors; weight by estimated reach only when the reach number is
sourced. In this repo, keep the factor columns in the snapshot so the
score is recomputable.

## Sentiment

Positive (praise, recommendation, a success story): amplify and reuse.
Neutral (factual): the baseline. Negative (criticism, a problem):
investigate and respond through a person. Mixed: analyse each driver.

## Benchmarks

| Metric | Average | Good | Excellent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| pitch response rate | 5 to 10 percent | 15 to 20 | 25 plus |
| coverage conversion | 2 to 5 percent | 8 to 12 | 15 plus |
| positive sentiment | 60 to 70 percent | 75 to 85 | 90 plus |
| message pull-through | 40 to 50 percent | 60 to 70 | 80 plus |
| tier-1 share of coverage | 10 to 15 percent | 20 to 30 | 35 plus |

## Attribution

Direct: UTM links in coverage, a dedicated landing page, "how did you
hear about us", first touch in the CRM. Correlated: traffic spikes on
coverage dates, brand search lift, mention velocity, pipeline timing.

## Do not report

Advertising value equivalency; raw impressions; total potential reach;
pitch volume as a result; follower counts.

## Monthly report shape

A two-to-three-sentence summary; coverage metrics (placements, tier
split, share of voice, sentiment); the top three pieces with context;
business effect (traffic, leads, pipeline where sourced); activity in one
line; upcoming opportunities.
