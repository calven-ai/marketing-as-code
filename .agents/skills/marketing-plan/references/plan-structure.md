# Plan structure and budget rules

Condensed from the source above (a twelve-month SaaS plan generator);
the sections and rules that fit a plan kept as a project brief in this
repo. The source's funding-stage figures are US-market, 2025; treat them
as orientation.

## Sections the brief carries

1. Summary: the three bets, the first 90 days, the twelve-month outcome.
2. Strategic frame: category, ICP, business model, voice rules, each
   pointing at the `strategy/` file rather than restating it.
3. Current state: team, budget lines, channels that work, do not, or are
   untested, and stuck initiatives.
4. One section per funnel stage that must move (acquisition, activation,
   retention, referral, revenue): the programs, the metric, the owner.
5. The 90-day roadmap in four phases: unblock, foundation, velocity,
   compound, with owners per week.
6. The twelve-month outlook by quarter, tied to what each budget
   milestone unlocks.
7. Measurement: the north-star metric, leading indicators, who owns each
   number, and the open decisions.

Every program names the funnel stage it serves; that is what forces
sequencing instead of a channel list.

## Budget

- Revenue-based: marketing at 5 to 40 percent of ARR depending on stage.
- Goal-based: new ARR divided by (average revenue per customer times
  twelve), times CAC, divided by annual retention.
- Stage anchors from the source, monthly: bootstrapped 0 to 2k (organic
  only); seed closed 5 to 15k (first paid tests, first hire); seed
  deployed 20 to 50k; Series A 50 to 150k; Series B and up 150k plus.
- Keep 10 to 20 percent for experiments, so the next channel is being
  tested before the current one plateaus.
- Blended CAC counts salaries, content, tools and retainers, not only ad
  spend.

## Growth shape

Growth is stacked S-curves, not a hockey stick. Split effort 70/20/10:
what works now, the next wave, experiments. Watch leading indicators
weekly for the plateau. Start the next curve before the current one
flattens.

## What every plan must state, or list as an open decision

Monthly spend by line; the team and who does marketing today; blended
CAC; average revenue per customer, retention and lifetime value; each
channel's status; the growth phase; upcoming budget milestones; an owner
per move. Unknown means "open decision", never a guess.

## Signs of a weak plan

Tactics with no sequence; more work than the team can do; paid budget
assumed before it exists; "build community" without a program; ignoring
what the team already tried; padding.
