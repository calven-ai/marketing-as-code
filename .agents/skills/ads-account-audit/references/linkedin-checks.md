# LinkedIn Ads audit checks

Condensed from the LinkedIn B2B playbook above and the audit checklist in
thatrebeccarae's LinkedIn Ads skill (MIT). Same scoring as the Google
list: pass, fail, unknown, not applicable, with the evidence line.

## The ten most frequent findings, in order

1. Audience Expansion left on (off for B2B lead gen).
2. LinkedIn Audience Network left on (off for B2B lead gen).
3. Audiences too small to deliver.
4. Fewer than 4 active ads per campaign.
5. Campaigns under about 10 results a week (starved).
6. Stale creative, 3 months or older.
7. No retargeting audiences created (they only start collecting from the
   day they are created; build every one before launch).
8. Lead quality never reconciled against the CRM.
9. Brand and geo budgets mixed in one campaign (keep the US separate, it
   is the most expensive).
10. Everything on automated bidding indefinitely.

## Checks by group

**Structure.** One objective and one audience per campaign; naming follows
`data/ontology/naming.md`; audiences split by intent, then persona, then
region or company size, then seniority; company size by employee count in
two bands to start.

**Targeting.** Sizes in range: sponsored content 50k minimum, 100k to 300k
recommended; message ads 15k minimum; lead gen forms 50k minimum; ABM
company lists 300 minimum, 500 to 1,000 recommended; retargeting 1k to 5k
per segment, under 300 will not deliver. Cold prospecting needs about 15k
as a floor. Two or three targeting layers at most; job title and seniority
are mutually exclusive, and function plus seniority roughly triples the
audience (then needs weekly negative titles). Existing customers excluded.

**Creative.** Format fits the objective (document ads for lead gen, 1080 x
1350 portrait, 5 to 7 slides; single image 1200 x 627; video 15 to 30 s
with captions). Intro text under 150 characters. At least 4 active ads.
Refreshed inside 4 to 6 weeks; anything past 3 months fails.

**Conversion tracking.** Insight Tag installed; conversion events
configured; a 30-day click window stated in reports; lead-form leads
matched to CRM outcomes at least monthly.

**Budget and bidding.** Week 1 on automated bidding to learn the CPC, then
manual CPC about 20% below the learned average; small retargeting and ABM
audiences stay automated. Daily budget large enough for the audience.
Scaling decided on penetration: under 25% raise, 25 to 35% hold, over 35%
add audiences. Doubling budget grows penetration 50 to 70%, not 100%.

## Benchmarks to label a number, not to target

CTR sponsored content over 0.8% good, under 0.4% poor; click-through to
landing page 0.30 to 0.55% cold, 0.80 to 1.30% retargeting; CPM 33 to 65;
CPC 8 to 22 or more cold; cost per lead gen form 50 to 200; cost per
website form 200 to 500; form fill rate over 8%; conversation ads 35 to
50% open, 2 to 5% CTR. Rising spend with flat reach means outbid or weak
creative.
