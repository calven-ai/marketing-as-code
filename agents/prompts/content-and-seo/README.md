# Content and SEO

Every piece needs four things you rarely have on hand: who it's for, the
words buyers use, the approved message and proof, and what already exists
so you don't write it twice. The repo holds all four: `strategy/`,
`content/` with its frontmatter, `data/seo/keywords.csv` and the buyer
prompts in `data/seo/prompts.csv`. These prompts work across them. Rank
tracking and the CMS stay in your tools.

| Use case | What it gets you | Skills it leans on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Content briefs](content-briefs.md) | A brief a writer or agency can run with, built on the persona and the keyword | `/content-brief`, `/new-content` |
| [Editorial planning](editorial-planning.md) | The quarter's plan from gaps in keywords, buyer prompts, personas and the inventory | `/content-strategy`, `/content-calendar`, `/keyword-cluster` |
| [Content audit and refresh](content-audit-and-refresh.md) | Stale, decaying and off-message pieces, ranked for a refresh | `/content-decay-monitor`, `/content-inventory` |
| [Comparison pages](comparison-pages.md) | Honest "vs" and alternatives pages that hold up in front of a buyer | `/comparison-page`, `/battlecard` |
| [Answer pages](answer-pages.md) | Pages AI answer engines cite, built from the questions buyers ask | `/aeo-page-optimize`, `/brand-monitor`, `/prompt-set-builder` |
