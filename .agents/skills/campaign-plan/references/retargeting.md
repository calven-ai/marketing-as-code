# Retargeting audiences and the message each one gets

Condensed from the paid-ads skill above. A campaign plan lists the
audiences to build before launch; platforms only collect from the day an
audience exists, so a missing audience is data lost for good.

## Audiences to create before the first ad runs

| Audience | Built from | Window | Message |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Site visitors, top of funnel | blog and content pages | 1 to 7 days | educational, social proof; higher frequency is fine |
| Engaged visitors, mid funnel | pricing, features, comparison pages | 7 to 30 days | case studies, demo, proof for the claim they saw |
| High intent, bottom of funnel | trial signups, abandoned forms, demo page | 30 to 90 days | objection handling, urgency, a different offer; 1 to 2 a week |
| Ad engagers | video viewers, document ad openers, lead-form openers | 30 to 90 days | the next step of the argument, never the same ad |
| Customer match | CRM list, per `data/ontology/` and the PII rule | ongoing | expansion only, or exclude |

Exclude existing customers and recent converters (7 to 14 days) from
every acquisition audience.

## Never re-show the same offer

The most common reason someone did not convert is that the offer was
not right for them. Retarget with a different angle, in this order of
priority:

1. Objection-handling ad: the top reasons people did not act, in their
   own words (from lost-deal notes, support, sales calls), as the
   headline.
2. Proof carousel: testimonials and numbers that support the claim of the
   original ad.
3. Other offers: the best-performing ads for a different entry point
   (guide, webinar, template) to the same audience.
4. Value-first offer: an audit, assessment or tool that is useful whether
   or not they buy.

## Structure and budget

- Testing split: about 70% of paid budget on proven campaigns, 30% on new
  audiences and creative.
- Scale in steps of about 20% with 3 to 5 days between steps; a 30% jump
  resets the platform's learning.
- One naming pattern across platforms, the slug from
  `data/ontology/naming.md` in every campaign name, so `ads-performance`
  can join spend to the project.
- Headline mirroring: the ad headline that wins is the landing page H1
  (`landing-page` skill), which the source credits with 15 to 20% more
  conversions from removed message mismatch.
- If CPA is high, check the landing page before the targeting; if CTR is
  low, the creative; if CPM is high, the audience is too narrow.
