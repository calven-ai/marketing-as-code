# What makes a story, and what PR is for

Condensed from the source above. Read it before deciding whether a
release is the right vehicle.

## The premise

PR multiplies distribution; it does not replace it. Coverage yields
backlinks, legitimacy and a surface that answer engines cite, not direct
conversions. The story is never the product; it is the trend, the data,
the conflict or the human, and the product is evidence inside it.

## Three angles that work

1. The founding story: the origin, the founder's reason.
2. David versus Goliath: the underdog against an incumbent way of doing
   things.
3. An enemy: a broken system or practice, never a named competitor.

Data stories, milestones with a narrative, and a fresh take on a trending
topic all fit inside those frames.

## Four modes of PR

| Mode | What | Effort | Speed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| reactive (newsjacking) | a point of view on trending news | low to medium | hours to days |
| proactive (pitching) | a media list and original stories | high | 2 to 8 weeks |
| inbound (source requests) | answering journalists' queries | low | days to weeks |
| owned (press page) | journalist self-service | one-time | n/a |

## Press page essentials

A copy-ready company paragraph; founder bios and headshots; a logo pack in
light and dark; product screenshots; recent coverage; founding date,
headcount and funding if disclosed; a press email, not a form; recent
releases; and a line promising a 24-hour response. In this repo the copy
lives in `content/` with `channel: web` and the assets in `brand/`.

## When PR is worth doing

A real story (proprietary data, a strong opinion, a milestone, a sharp
customer narrative, a trending angle); founder or executive time for
quotes and interviews; a clear destination for the reader. Skip it
pre-launch with no story beyond existing, without four to six weeks of
pitching capacity, or without a clear ICP for the coverage.

## What to measure

Placements per month, the domain rating of the placements, referral
traffic from coverage, brand search lift, citation by answer engines, and
sales conversations that mention an article. Never advertising value
equivalency. The `coverage-tracker` skill owns these numbers.
