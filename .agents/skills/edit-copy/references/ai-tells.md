# AI writing tells

Condensed from the source above and rewritten for this repo. A tell is
not a crime; a cluster of them is what readers notice.

## The first thing to cut

Em-dashes. Use a comma for an aside, a colon for an explanation,
parentheses for a supplement; at most one per page, and none in this
repo's own prose.

## Verbs

Delve, leverage, optimise, utilise, facilitate, foster, bolster,
underscore, unveil, navigate, streamline, enhance, endeavour, ascertain,
elucidate. Replace with explore, use, improve, help, support, show,
handle, simplify, try, find out, explain.

## Adjectives

Robust, comprehensive, pivotal, crucial, vital, transformative,
cutting-edge, groundbreaking, innovative, seamless, intricate, nuanced,
multifaceted, holistic. Usually delete; sometimes "strong", "full",
"new", "smooth", "detailed".

## Transitions and phrases

Furthermore, moreover, notwithstanding, that being said, at its core, to
put it simply, it is worth noting that, in the realm of, with that in
mind, this begs the question, in essence.

Openers: "In today's fast-paced world", "In today's digital age", "In an
era of", "In the ever-evolving landscape of", "Let's delve into",
"Imagine a world where".

Closers: "In conclusion", "To sum up", "By doing X, you can achieve Y",
"At the end of the day".

Academic: shed light on, pave the way for, a myriad of, a plethora of,
paramount, pertaining to, prior to, subsequent to, in light of, with
respect to, in terms of, the fact that.

## Structures

- Three-example "whether ... whether ... or whether" constructions.
- "It's not just X, it's also Y."
- Elaborate metaphors that explain nothing.
- Sentences opening on a gerund: "By understanding X, ...".
- Intensifiers that add no meaning: absolutely, certainly, undoubtedly,
  truly, genuinely and their kin.

## Self-check

Read it aloud; would a colleague say this sentence? Do sentence lengths
vary? Do the listed words cluster? Does every intensifier earn its
place? Then hand the piece to `review`.
