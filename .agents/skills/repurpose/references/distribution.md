# Distribution: half-lives, ORB and the stagger

Condensed from the source above and rewritten for this repo.

## The loop

Create one flagship piece with distribution hooks built in; atomise it
into channel formats; distribute across owned, rented and borrowed
channels, always routing back to owned; watch the responses; feed what
you learned into the next flagship. Consistent distributors pull away
from one-off publishers.

## Own, rent, borrow

| Layer | Channels | Job | Control |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Borrowed | podcasts, guest posts, partners, PR | discovery | none |
| Rented | social platforms, ad networks, marketplaces | reach | the content only |
| Owned | email, blog, community, the product | conversion, retention | full |

Use borrowed and rented reach to bring strangers into owned channels.
The source's warning: most teams spend about 90% of effort on channels
they do not own.

## Half-lives

| Channel | How long a post lives | So |
| --- | --- | --- |
| X | minutes to hours | frequency, reposting, threads |
| Instagram, Facebook | about a day | frequent, ephemeral |
| LinkedIn | about a day, longer when it lands | fewer, higher-effort posts |
| Short video | days to weeks | resurfaced by the algorithm |
| YouTube | months to years | searchable titles |
| Blog | years | evergreen framing, long tail |
| Email | one shot | archive and reuse |

Short half-life rewards repetition; long half-life rewards depth.

## Atomisation checklist per flagship

- Three to five standalone posts from the subheads and key points
- One thread or carousel
- Two to four short clips when the source is video or audio
- One or two quote or stat graphics (a number needs its snapshot)
- One email to the owned list linking the flagship
- A reshare schedule across the piece's life

## Create once, distribute twice

Design the source for cutting: modular sections, subheads that work as
posts on their own, pull quotes and graphics chosen before drafting. One
strong piece cut ten ways reaches more than eleven mediocre native
posts.

## Stagger

Day 1 the flagship; days 1 to 2 the thread and the LinkedIn post; days
3 to 4 the carousel and the newsletter mention; days 5 to 7 short video;
week 2 quote cards and discussion posts. Reshare evergreen pieces every
three to six months.
