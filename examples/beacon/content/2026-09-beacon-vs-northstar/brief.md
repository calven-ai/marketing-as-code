# Brief: Beacon vs Northstar

- **Project:** projects/q3-launch/comparison-page
- **Owner:** Ana Ruiz
- **Channel:** web
- **Target date:** 2026-09-22

## Why this piece

Most prospects already have Northstar's status page, and "we already have
one" is the first objection in nearly every deal. The page answers it for
the Head of Support at the consideration stage, and is where paid social
points from 2026-09-23. A reader should leave knowing which tool fits
their team, including when that is Northstar.

## The argument

Northstar is a monitoring suite with a good status page for engineers.
Beacon is for the support team that has to talk to customers: voice
templates, audience segments, a report the customer forwards. Many teams
keep both. Fairness is the argument: say plainly when Northstar is enough.
