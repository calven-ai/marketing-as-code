# On-page checklist for one page

Condensed from the on-page and content sections of
coreyhaines31/marketingskills `seo-audit`. Check every row against the
page; the report lists only rows that fail, with the fix.

## Title

- Unique on the site.
- Primary keyword near the start; brand at the end.
- 50 to 60 characters so it shows whole in results.
- Gives a reason to click, without stuffing.

## Meta description

- Unique; 150 to 160 characters.
- Contains the keyword; states the value; ends with a reason to act.
- Not auto-generated from the first paragraph.

## Headings

- Exactly one H1, containing the keyword.
- H1 to H2 to H3 without skipped levels.
- Headings describe the section; none used for styling only.
- Question-form H2s where the SERP shows people-also-ask boxes.

## Body

- Keyword in the first 100 words, naturally.
- Related terms and entities the winning pages cover.
- Depth matched to the intent: a "what is" page is short and direct, a
  comparison is a table, a how-to is steps.
- Answers the intent better than the current top 10, not just longer.
- No thin, doorway or near-duplicate content; no keyword stuffing.

## Images

- Descriptive file names; alt text that describes the image (and carries
  the keyword only when true).
- Compressed, modern format, lazy loaded below the fold, responsive.

## Internal links

- Inbound: the pillar or category page and two or three related pages
  link here with descriptive anchors.
- Outbound: links to the pillar and to related pages; no broken links; a
  reasonable count, not a footer pile.
- Anchor text varied; not the exact keyword every time.

## Keyword targeting

- One clear primary keyword; title, H1 and URL agree.
- No other page on the site targets the same keyword (cannibalisation).
- The page belongs to a cluster (`keyword-cluster`) and links within it.

## Content quality signals

- First-hand experience shown: screenshots, numbers, named examples.
- Author named, with relevant expertise; date published and updated.
- Sources cited for claims and statistics.
- Specific rather than generic; would a reader who knows the topic learn
  something.

## Output per failing row

Element, current value, proposed value, reason (which check, what the
SERP showed), effort.
