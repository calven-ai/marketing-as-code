# Benchmarks for judging a sequence

Condensed from the source above (2024 to 2025 figures). These are for
judging a draft and a first batch; the team's own rates come from
`data/email/snapshots/`, never from here.

| Metric | Average | Good | Excellent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| open rate | 28 percent | 40 to 45 | 50 plus |
| reply rate | 4 to 6 percent | 5 to 10 | 10 to 15 (best in class 15 to 25) |
| positive replies, share of replies | about 48 percent | 55 to 60 | 62 to 65 |
| meetings booked | 0.5 to 1 percent | 1 to 2 | 2.3 plus |
| bounce rate | 7.5 percent | under 4 | under 2 |

Funnel shape at average performance: 500 sends, 100 opens, 25 replies,
8 positive, 4 meetings, 1 customer.

## What moves replies, in order

1. Hook type: a timeline or trigger hook beats a generic problem hook by
   more than three times.
2. Personalisation depth: up to 250 percent more replies.
3. Brevity: 25 to 75 words gets 83 percent more replies.
4. Targeting: batches of 50 or fewer get 2.8 times the reply rate.
5. The first follow-up: about 49 percent more replies.
6. Reading level: third to fifth grade gets 67 percent more replies.
7. Send day: Thursday peaks.

Reply rates fell from 7 to 8 percent (2020 to 2022) to 4 to 6 percent
(2024 to 2025); judge a batch against the current band.

## By audience

C-level replies slightly more often than others (6.4 versus 5.2 percent);
CTOs and founders about 7.6 percent; heads of sales 6.6. SaaS, financial
services and IT services are the hardest segments (around 3.5 percent).
North America 4.1, Europe 3.1, Asia-Pacific 2.8.
