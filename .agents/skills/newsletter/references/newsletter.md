# Newsletter shape, formats, subject lines, benchmarks

Condensed from the newsletter-management skill above. The source
recommends weekly; this repo's cadence is monthly per edition, so the
issue carries more and each item stays short.

## The shape of an issue

1. Opening: two or three sentences from a named person, with context
   for the month.
2. Feature: one story, framework or announcement, the reason the issue
   exists.
3. Items: five to ten, each a title, one line on why it matters, a link.
   Group them (what we published, what shipped, from the community).
4. Supporting sections when there is material: a number of the month,
   a customer quote, a question from the community.
5. Closing: sign-off and a P.S. (the P.S. is read; put the second-best
   thing there).

## Six formats to rotate

| Format | When | Time to write |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Curated roundup | a full month of published pieces | 30 to 45 min |
| Story-driven | one lesson worth a narrative | 45 to 60 min |
| Educational deep dive | a framework the team uses | 60 to 90 min |
| Interview or Q&A | a customer or expert, 5 to 10 questions | 90 to 120 min |
| Data-driven | a report in `reports/` worth sharing | 60 to 90 min |
| Personal update | what the team worked on, learned, and one recommendation | 30 to 45 min |

Prospect edition: roundup, deep dive or data. Customer edition: personal
update or story, plus what shipped.

## Sourcing filter

Include an item only if you would send it to one person directly, it
teaches something they can act on, the source is credible, it is timely,
and the issue adds a line of value beyond the link.

## Subject lines

Under 50 characters, about 35 for mobile. Shapes: "Issue <n>: <teaser>",
a direct value statement ("5 tools that changed our reporting"), a
question the feature answers. Preview text extends the subject; it never
repeats it.

## Sending

Same day and time each cycle. Tuesday, then Thursday, then Wednesday;
avoid Monday and Friday. The segment (prospects, customers, both) is
chosen by a person in the tool.

## Benchmarks, as labels only

| Metric | Good | Great |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open rate | 30 to 40% | over 40% |
| Click rate | 3 to 5% | over 5% |
| Unsubscribes per issue | under 0.5% | |
| List growth per month | 5 to 10% | |

Judge an issue against the team's own issues in `data/email/snapshots/`
first. The reliable growth lever is consistent quality, not a tactic.
