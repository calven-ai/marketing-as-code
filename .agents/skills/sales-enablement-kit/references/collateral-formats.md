# Sales collateral formats

Condensed from the sales enablement skill in marketingskills. One shape
per format; the skill picks one and fills it from `strategy/`.

## Principles

Reps need information in three seconds, not thirty: scannable, in the
rep's words rather than marketing's, specific to the situation. Every
claim ties to a business outcome (revenue, efficiency, risk), not a
feature.

## Sales deck (10 to 12 slides)

Problem → cost of the problem → what changed in the market → the approach
→ product walkthrough → proof → a case study → implementation timeline →
ROI → pricing → the ask. Customise by buyer: technical buyers get
architecture and security; economic buyers get ROI and payback; champions
get material they can forward upward.

## One-pager or leave-behind

Problem statement; the solution in two sentences; three differentiators;
one proof point (a number, a named customer); a clear call to action.
Readable in thirty seconds after a meeting or at a stand.

## Objection handling doc

Grouped by category: price, timing, competition, authority, status quo,
technical. Per objection: what they say; the concern underneath; the
response approach; the evidence; a follow-up question that keeps the
conversation moving. The objections come from win/loss, not from
imagination.

## Demo scripts and talk tracks

One per call type: discovery, first demo, technical deep dive, executive
overview. Sections: opening, discovery recap (their words back to them),
solution walkthrough, planned interaction points, close. Keep a "do not
show" list for the weaknesses in `strategy/product-brief.md`.

## ROI calculator and value proposition

Inputs: their current metrics. Calculations: time and cost saved, revenue
impact. Outputs: payback period, three-year value, phrased per persona.
The formula and every default come from a snapshot or the ontology, or
the calculator ships with the field empty.

## Playbook and persona cards

Playbook: buyer profiles, qualification criteria, discovery questions,
competitive positioning, the objection library. Persona card: role, goals,
pains, evaluation criteria, what proof they need; all from
`strategy/personas.md`, one card per persona.

## Partner kit (added for this repo)

What we are in one line; who we are for and not for; how the partner
earns (referral, resell, integration); the joint value proposition; two
or three proof points; co-marketing assets available; who to contact.
The partner reads it without us in the room, so it stands alone.

## Before writing

Check the strategy context; gather the value proposition, the sales
motion, the collateral requested and what already exists. Test drafts
with the best reps; use the prospect's own terms and data when known.
