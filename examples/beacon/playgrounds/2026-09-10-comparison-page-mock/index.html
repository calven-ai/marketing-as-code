Beacon vs Northstar: different jobs

Northstar tells engineering what is down. Beacon tells customers what it means for them. Many of our customers run both. Here is how to tell which one you need.

Which one fits

Northstar alone is right when

  • Engineering buys and runs the tools
  • Your customers are developers who want the uptime graph
  • One message for everyone is the right message
  • The page you already pay for does the job

Add Beacon when

  • The first update is late: median 48 seconds on Beacon
  • Tickets come from customers who were fine: 31 percent fewer at a design-tool customer
  • The renewal call opens with the outage: 64 percent open the report
