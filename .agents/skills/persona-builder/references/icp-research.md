# Persona dimensions, pain and objection categories

Condensed from the ICP research skill in claude-marketing. Use the
categories to sort what the transcripts say, so two personas are written
against the same grid and can be compared.

## Three dimensions of a persona

| Dimension | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| Demographics | Job title, seniority, department, company size, industry, location |
| Psychographics | Daily responsibilities, the KPIs they are measured on, frustrations, career goals, where they get information |
| Buying behavior | Who influences the decision, who approves budget, the process type, evaluation criteria, timeline |

## Pain points: five categories, one priority formula

Time, money, risk, quality, growth. Priority = frequency × intensity, both
read from the sources, not guessed. A pain nobody mentioned unprompted
ranks below one that three people raised on their own.

## Objections: five categories and the response each needs

| Objection | Underlying concern | Response shape |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | Value not yet believed | An ROI figure from a snapshot, or a customer's own number |
| Time | Implementation effort | The migration path, time to first value |
| Trust | Will it work for us | Social proof from a comparable company |
| Need | Status quo feels fine | The cost of the current way, in their words |
| Authority | Not the decision maker | Material the champion can forward upward |

## Buying triggers

External: funding, a leadership change, competitor pressure, a regulation.
Internal: a missed target, team friction, scaling pain, a tool being
sunset. Record the trigger for each closed-won deal in the CRM snapshot;
it is the most reliable persona field there is.

## Community research

Find where the persona talks (Reddit, Discord, LinkedIn groups, Quora,
niche forums, review platforms), search for the pain in their words, and
classify posters (the persona, an adjacent role, a vendor) before quoting
anyone. Quotes go into `memory/knowledge/` as customer language, tagged
with the source.

## From persona to messaging angle

Each pain becomes a headline candidate, each proof point the support, each
trigger a moment to reach them. Hand that to `messaging-house`; the persona
file holds the evidence, not the copy.

## Principles

Real language over marketing language; pain over features; be specific;
validate against community data rather than assumption; one profile per
distinct persona, never a blend.
