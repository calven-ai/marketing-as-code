# Social formats and hooks

Condensed from the source above and rewritten for this repo. Cadence
numbers are the source's defaults; the team's calendar decides.

## Per platform

| Platform | Cadence | Formats that carry | What lands |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn | 3 to 5 a week | document posts, carousels, text with breaks | B2B insight, lessons, stories |
| X | several a day | threads, short takes | commentary, real-time replies, contrarian takes |
| Instagram | 1 to 2 a day plus stories | reels, carousels | visual brands |
| TikTok | 1 to 4 a day | short raw video | awareness, younger audiences |
| Facebook | 1 to 2 a day | groups, native video | communities, local |

## Hooks

- Curiosity: "I was wrong about {belief}." "The real reason {outcome}
  happens isn't what you think."
- Story: "Last week, {unexpected thing} happened." "Three years ago I
  {then}. Today, {now}."
- Value: "How to {outcome} (without {pain}):" "{N} {things} that
  {outcome}:" "Stop {mistake}. Do this instead:"
- Contrarian: "Unpopular opinion: {claim}" "{Common advice} is wrong.
  Here's why:" "I stopped {practice} and {result}."

A hook is a promise; the body must pay it.

## Atoms from long-form

| Atom | Shape | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quotable moment | a bold line, 15 to 60 seconds | X, LinkedIn, TikTok |
| Story arc | setup, conflict, resolution in 60 to 90 seconds | reels, shorts |
| Tactical tip | one how-to or framework in 30 to 60 seconds | LinkedIn, shorts |
| Contrarian take | one opinion that invites debate | X, LinkedIn |
| Data callout | one surprising number, with its source | LinkedIn carousel, X |
| Behind the scenes | unpolished, real | stories, TikTok |

Per episode of audio or video the source expects three to five clips,
one or two LinkedIn posts, one thread, one carousel, one newsletter
section.

## LinkedIn carousels

Pick the narrative before the slides: value stack, problem and proof,
hack list, rant, demo walkthrough. Cover slide, six to eight content
slides with at most three lines each, a CTA slide with attribution.

## Short video

Three seconds to stop the scroll: visual hook, spoken hook and text
overlay together. Problem, agitate, solution, CTA in 15 to 30 seconds;
lists at one item every five to eight seconds; tutorials show the result
first. Subtitles always, two lines at most, three to five words a line.

## What to schedule and what to post live

Schedule core posts, threads, carousels and evergreen reshares; post
live for commentary on news, replies and engagement. Keep one to two
weeks queued, review weekly, leave gaps. Nothing here is scheduled by a
skill; `publish` stages, a person activates.

## Weekly read

Top three and bottom three posts and why, follower trend, engagement
trend, best times from the data in `data/social/snapshots/`, never from
memory. Comments outrank likes; saves and shares outrank both.
