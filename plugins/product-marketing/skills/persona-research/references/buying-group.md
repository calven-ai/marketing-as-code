# Researching a whole buying group

Open this when the request is for every persona, not one.

## How many and which

- Four to six personas that cover the people who evaluate, champion, approve and use the product, one per distinct role.
- Work the group out from the ICP and the site. Typically: an economic buyer (signs), an executive sponsor or champion (drives it internally), a technical evaluator (vets the fit), and the primary end users (live in it daily).
- In an enterprise or regulated deal, add the gates that can kill it: security, procurement, legal or finance. Type them Stakeholder, not Buyer.
- Aim for a balanced mix of Buyer, Stakeholder and User. If the ICP implies fewer distinct roles, four beats padding; if it implies several distinct end-user roles, go to six.

## What makes a set strong

1. **Source diversity.** Each persona leans on mostly vendor-neutral sources; no single source, least of all the company's own pages or one case study, dominates the set.
2. **Distinct deal motions.** Adjacent roles differ by what only that role owns. If two personas share most of their pains and hooks, it is one persona written twice: sharpen or merge.
3. **Clear deal roles.** Each persona states whether it signs, sponsors, validates or can block, and no two personas hold the same deal role. An end user is usually a champion or a blocker, not a buyer; say so.
4. **Tight, not exhaustive.** Two to four KPIs per persona. Every cell earns its place.
5. **Coverage of the ICP.** Every ICP segment that buys has its buyer in the set.

## Order of work

1. Read the ICP, the site and any first-party material once, for the whole group.
2. Decide the group and write a one-line brief per persona: role, type, deal role, and what only it owns.
3. Research and write each persona in turn with the main procedure.
4. Compare the finished set side by side: deal roles do not conflict, no near-duplicates, every ICP segment covered.
5. Return the set with a one-table summary first: `| Persona | Type | Buying role | Role in the deal | Owns what no other persona owns |`.
