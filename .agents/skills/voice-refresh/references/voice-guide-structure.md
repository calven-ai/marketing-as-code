# Voice guide structure and the consistency audit

Condensed from the brand voice guidelines skill in claude-marketing. The
audit checklist is what the refresh runs; the structure says what a full
guide holds, so a gap in `brand/voice.md` is visible.

## Voice and tone

Voice is who the brand is and does not change. Tone adapts to the
situation: urgency, audience, channel. A guide that only lists adjectives
has voice without tone; the context table is where most drift shows.

## Building or rebuilding a guide, five steps

1. Content audit: ten to twenty pieces across channels; which adjectives
   fit consistently; how the audience would describe the writer.
2. Voice attributes: three to five, each on a spectrum with the failure
   mode named (confident but not arrogant; friendly but not familiar).
3. Archetype: one of sage, creator, hero, jester, caregiver, rebel,
   explorer, ruler, everyperson, as a shorthand for the personality, not
   as a rule.
4. Tone matrix: how confidence, formality, humour and empathy shift across
   website, blog, social, support, error messages, legal and crisis.
5. Messaging framework: promise, value propositions, taglines, elevator
   pitch, boilerplate (in this repo those live in `strategy/messaging.md`
   and the voice guide points at them).

## Writing standards worth deciding once

Oxford comma or not; contractions; exclamation marks (rarely); sentence
case for headings; numerals from ten upward; how product names and
features are capitalised. Vocabulary: "use" not "utilise", "collaboration"
not "synergy"; specific over generic; no labels for people.

## The consistency audit

- Sample three pages or pieces per channel.
- Score each voice attribute one to five per piece.
- Mark the outliers and what makes them off.
- Search for forbidden terms.
- Check the tone of each piece against the context it sits in.
- Read aloud: does it sound like one person?

Findings feed the do and don't table as before-and-after pairs taken from
real pieces, which is the most useful section a guide has.

## What the finished guide holds

Identity statement; voice attributes with examples; tone matrix; a
pointer to the messaging framework; style decisions; channel-specific
rules; before-and-after rewrites for each principle; the banned list; a
glossary.
