# Persona template

The profile, the description, eight canvas sections in this order, and Words they use. Keep every section and column; a section with nothing grounded says so in one line.

```markdown
# Persona: <role name>

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Buyer, User or Stakeholder |
| Buying role | Economic buyer, Decision maker, Champion, Exec sponsor, Technical buyer, End user, Influencer or Blocker |
| Role title | <the title as it appears on job postings> |
| Seniority | <IC, Manager, Director, VP, C-level> |
| Department | <function> |
| Segment | <the ICP segment this persona sits in> |
| Role in the deal | <signs, sponsors, validates or can block, and on what> |

<Description: one or two sentences. Who they are, what they own, their top pain or goal. No citations here.>

## Company objectives / initiatives
| Objective or initiative | How this persona advances it |
| --- | --- |

| --- | --- | --- |

| Pain | Description | Impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
(the buyer's world, in their words; impact High, Medium or Low)

| --- | --- |

```

## What each section must show

| Section | Strong | Weak |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Objectives | Concrete company objectives the role moves, and how | "Drive growth" |
| Goals & KPIs | A metric, a definition and a target or direction | A list of ten metrics; unlabelled invented targets |
| Pains | Specific, impact-rated, in the buyer's language | "Lack of visibility"; the product's pitch restated as a pain |
| Gains | Tied to a business or personal outcome | "Efficiency" |
| Jobs to be done | The job named and typed | Features written as jobs |
| Objections | A real doubt with a grounded response | A strawman the product easily beats |
| Messaging hooks | A specific line tied to a named pain or gain | A tagline that fits any persona |
| Watering holes | Named, checkable places | "Social media", "industry events" |

## Sourcing notes

- Job postings for the role are a strong neutral source for responsibilities, KPIs and tools.
- Practitioner communities, forums and podcasts give the buyer's words for pains and objections.
- Analyst category pages give the frame the role buys within.
- The company's own pages give its language for the role, not the buyer's. Use them, but never as the main source for pains or objections.
- A quote from a transcript is attributed to a role and the file ("a support team lead, call of 2026-10-14"), never a person's name unless the user supplied it.
