# Competitor watch

- **Date:** 2026-10-01 · **Prepared by:** `competitor-watch` with Ana Ruiz
- **Question:** "What changed at Northstar and Pagewise in September?"

## Answer

Northstar shipped subscriber groups for status pages on 2026-09-17, in
beta and on the Business tier only: an update can go to a chosen group of
subscribers. That is the first thing they have shipped close to our
audience segments, and the Northstar card's second landmine ("a different
message to the customers who were affected") no longer lands as cleanly.
Pagewise is new to the watch this month; its pages are the baseline.

## Northstar

- **Product and changelog:** subscriber groups, 2026-09-17. Groups are
  chosen by hand per update; no segments by region, plan or feature, and
  no report. What it likely means: the bundled page is moving toward our
  ground, slowly, and only on their top tier.
- **Homepage and pricing:** unchanged. Status pages still bundled from
  the Team plan.
- **Search:** we rank 9 for "northstar alternative" with the comparison
  page eight days old.

## Pagewise

Baseline, first run. Starter 29 USD a month and Business 99, public and
monthly; self-serve, no sales call. Shipped incident templates on
2026-09-10, five starter texts: the nearest thing they have to our voice
templates. No audience segments, no post-incident report.
