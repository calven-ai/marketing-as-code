# Fit ranking and messaging needs

Open this file at steps 8 and 9, after the market map is finished.

## Fit criteria

Judge each mapped vertical on five criteria. Write one line of reasoning per criterion and cite where it comes from (the map, the product brief, the ICP, the CRM export).

| Criterion | Question | Strong | Weak |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Need match | Does the vertical's driver theme map to something the product does today? | The driver names the product's core job | Only a side feature touches it |
| Market pull | How do size and momentum combine? | large or mid, and accelerating | niche and cooling |
| Proof | Does the company have customers, case studies or deals in this vertical? | Named in the user's inputs | None stated (never assume) |
| Blockers | Does the vertical require something the product lacks (a deployment option, a certification, a channel, an integration)? | None found | A hard requirement unmet |
| Evidence | How well is the vertical's map entry grounded? | high | low |

Overall fit: **Strong** (need match strong, no hard blocker), **Possible** (need match strong but a gap in proof or a soft blocker), **Weak** (need match weak, or a hard blocker), **Excluded** (the ICP disqualifies it; list it, do not rank it). Rank Strong before Possible before Weak; within a level, market pull then evidence break ties. A vertical with low evidence is never ranked first without saying so.

## Messaging needs (top three to five verticals)

| Field | What to write |
| --- | --- |
| Trigger | The event that makes a company in this vertical look for a solution now |
| Their words | The terms the vertical uses for the problem and the job, from sources you read (job posts, industry press, customer stories) |
| Proof they need | The evidence a buyer in this vertical expects before believing the claim: a customer in the same vertical, a certification, a number |
| Compliance and procurement asks | Requirements that gate a deal here, only those you found sourced |
| Likely objection | The most probable reason they say no, and whether the product can answer it honestly today |
| Message angle | One sentence on what the vertical page or campaign should lead with, built only on capabilities the product brief states |

If the company lacks the proof a vertical needs, say so: that gap is the first thing to fix before marketing to it.
