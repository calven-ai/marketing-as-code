## 2026-08-20: Paid social is paused until the comparison page exists

- **Decided by:** Ana Ruiz, with Tomas Berg
- **Source:** the Q3 launch status meeting of 2026-08-20
- **Context:** paid social sent traffic to the product page, where a visitor comparing us with Northstar found nothing to compare. Forty percent of the budget went to visitors who left in under ten seconds. The comparison page is the missing step; ads resume when it is live.
- **Follow-ups:** Ana writes the comparison page brief by 2026-09-05 (in-repo checklist, projects/q3-launch/status.md)

## 2026-06-12: We position as incident communication, not as a status page

- **Decided by:** Ana Ruiz, the CEO and the Head of Product
- **Source:** the positioning workshop of 2026-06-11 and 2026-06-12
- **Context:** "status page" is a feature buyers already have from their monitoring suite and a budget marketing never reaches. "Incident communication" names the support leader's job and the budget they own. The cost is a category nobody searches for yet; the category term stays in the keyword list so the product page still ranks for it.
- **Follow-ups:** none
