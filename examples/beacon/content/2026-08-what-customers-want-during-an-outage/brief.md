# Brief: What customers want during an outage

- **Project:** projects/q3-launch
- **Owner:** Tomas Berg
- **Channel:** blog
- **Target date:** 2026-08-28

## Why this piece

Advances the "right people, only" pillar for the Head of Support persona.
A reader should finish thinking "our status page is not enough" and click
through to the product page for audience segments.

## The argument

Customers do not want a status page during an outage; they want three
things: to hear it from you before they notice, to know whether it affects
them, and to get a report they can forward. Supporting points: the first
update matters more than the fix; the wrong audience creates the ticket
spike; the report is what the renewal conversation remembers.

## Keywords / distribution

Target keyword: "customer communication during outage" (informational, 210
per month). Distribution: LinkedIn from Ana's account, the Support Driven
community, the newsletter.

## Sources and raw material

The 48-second median from product analytics, the 31 percent ticket
reduction from the design-tool customer's own report, and three support
call transcripts from July.
