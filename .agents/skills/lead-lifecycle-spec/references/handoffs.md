# Handoffs: what travels with a lead and how each side is measured

Condensed from the source above; this skill uses the marketing-to-sales
node, with the later nodes noted so the spec does not contradict them.

## Every handoff has five parts

An owner, a context packet, an SLA, a trigger, and a measurement. A spec
missing any of the five will not hold.

## Marketing to sales

Lead types and their response promise:

| Lead | First response |
| --- | --- |
| hand-raiser (demo, pricing) | under 5 minutes; bypasses scoring |
| paid-ad conversion | under 3 minutes |
| partner referral | under 30 minutes |
| organic inbound | under 10 minutes |
| content or webinar lead | 3 to 4 days of nurture first; no premature call |
| product-qualified lead (PLG) | usage trigger; converts at 15 to 30 percent |

The context packet: firmographics, full engagement history, the score
split into fit and intent, the buying-group context, qualification notes
if an SDR spoke to them, enrichment done before routing.

The mutual promise: marketing delivers the agreed number of MQLs and
pipeline coverage (about four times the target); sales responds within
the SLA, makes 5 to 10 follow-up attempts, and dispositions the lead in
the CRM by the deadline. Enforcement: auto-escalation at 24 hours,
auto-reassignment, a weekly compliance report.

Scoring lifts MQL-to-SQL from 15 to 21 percent to 39 to 40 percent.

## Leading indicators of a broken handoff

Unworked leads above 10 percent (SDR overload or routing failure); MQL
rejection rising (ICP misalignment); time to kickoff above 14 days after a
win (sales-to-CS bottleneck).

## Measurement per handoff

| Handoff | Metrics | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| marketing to sales | speed to lead, MQL to SQL, unworked rate | under 5 minutes for hand-raisers, 25 to 35 percent, under 5 percent |
| sales to implementation | time to kickoff, packet completeness | under 7 days, above 90 percent |
| implementation to CS | time to first value, go-live rate | segment-dependent, above 85 percent |
| CS to sales (expansion) | expansion pipeline, handback time, NRR | growing, under 48 hours, 110 to 130 percent |

A receiving team scores each handoff 1 to 5 on completeness, promise
alignment, sentiment continuity, context and stakeholder accuracy;
reviewed weekly.

## Later nodes, in one line each

Sales to implementation: seven-part packet (deal history, measurable
success criteria, stakeholder map, every commitment made, risks,
technical requirements, timeline), kickoff within 48 to 72 hours. CS to
sales: an upsell keeps the champion and budget; a cross-sell into a new
buying group is a new deal with full discovery; ownership by ACV band.
