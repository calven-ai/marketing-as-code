# Demand generation

Every campaign starts with the same four questions: who you're targeting,
what they care about at this stage, what you say and what you can prove.
The answers sit in `strategy/` and `projects/`; the results sit in
`data/crm/`, `data/ads/`, `data/email/` and `reports/`. These prompts
connect the two so the brief, the copy and the retro all argue from the
same facts. Ad platforms and automation stay where they are.

| Use case | What it gets you | Skills it leans on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Campaign briefs](campaign-briefs.md) | The brief every writer, designer and agency works from | `/campaign-discovery`, `/campaign-plan` |
| [Email nurtures](email-nurtures.md) | Journey map, sequence and emails, checked against the lifecycle you already run | `/nurture-sequence`, `/lifecycle-map` |
| [Paid ads](paid-ads.md) | Ad angles in the buyer's words, tested on the personas before spend | `/ad-brief`, `/ads-performance`, `/ads-account-audit` |
| [ABM plays](abm-plays.md) | Accounts ranked by fit and heat, with a play per persona | `/target-account-list`, `/account-signals`, `/researcher` |
| [Campaign retros](campaign-retros.md) | Why a campaign landed or didn't, and what the playbook keeps | `/program-retro` |
