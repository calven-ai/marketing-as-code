# Rubrics

Section weight in brackets after the section name; criterion weight in the last column. A foundation's score is the weighted average of its section scores and its document-level group (the group weight is given with it). Map a team's own headings onto these sections before grading.

## Who owns which fact

| Document | Owns |
| --- | --- |
| ICP | Company size, industries, regions, buying triggers, disqualifiers, sales motion and deal shape |
| Personas | Buying roles, who brings it in and who signs, each role's pains, goals, objections in their own words, where they look for answers |
| Positioning | The alternatives buyers weigh, the category, unique attributes, proof and results, why now |
| Messaging | The narrative, value pillars, how objections are answered, boilerplate |

A gap rests with the document that owns the fact; ask about it once, there.
