# LinkedIn launch post: audience segments

Image: `stat.png` (48s) from kit.json.

---

Think about your last outage. Where did the tickets come from?

In most support queues we have looked at, about half came from customers
who were never affected. They saw the status page, or the email to
everyone, and wrote in to ask if they were next.

That is not a writing problem. It is an audience problem.

So we built audience segments into Beacon. Pick the regions, plans or
features an incident touches, and the update goes to those customers
only: on the status page, in the product, in the inbox. Everyone else
keeps working.

One design-tool customer cut incident tickets by 31 percent in the
quarter after they started doing this. Across Beacon, the median time to
a first update is 48 seconds.
