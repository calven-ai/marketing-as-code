# Claim, reframe or create

Open this in procedure step 7. Answer each test with evidence from the reading, then take the first
row whose conditions all hold.

## The tests

1. **Buyer use:** do buyers use the consensus label for this problem, in their own words and searches?
2. **Fit:** does the consensus definition describe what the product does, including its main differentiator?
3. **Budget:** does the label put the product in front of the buyer who owns the budget (per the positioning's target customer)?
4. **Standing:** is the company named in the category by assistants, analysts or review sites, or could it credibly be named next to the vendors that are?
5. **Funding:** can the company pay for educating a market (content, analyst briefings, a sustained campaign over years)? Ask the user if unknown.

## The decision

| Call | When | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Claim** | 1, 2 and 3 hold | Compete inside the consensus category and win on the differentiators. Use the consensus label verbatim. |
| **Reframe** | 1 holds, but 2 or 3 fails | Keep the label buyers know as the anchor and shift the frame: "<new frame>, not <familiar label>". The new frame must name the buyer's job. |
| **Create** | 1 fails for every existing label, or every existing frame makes the main differentiator invisible, and 5 holds | Name a new category. Buyers will not search it at first, so pair it with the label they do search. |
| **Claim, for now** | Create would be indicated but 5 fails | Claim the nearest consensus category and differentiate inside it; revisit when funding exists. |

Standing (test 4) does not decide the call; it sets the risk. Claiming a category where the company
is never named means competing for a place in answers and evaluations it does not yet have.

## Risks to state

- **Claim:** the differentiator reads as a feature of the leader's product.
- **Reframe:** buyers keep using the old label; the reframe must still rank and be named for it.
- **Create:** the label never catches on; answer engines and review sites keep filing the company under the old category; years of spend.

## Confidence

- **High:** all four voices read, and they agree.
- **Medium:** three voices read, or four with one disagreeing.
- **Low:** two or fewer voices read, or the claimed label is absent from every source.
