# Marketing leadership

You answer for the whole picture: what marketing delivered, what it should
do next and where the money goes. The repo holds the inputs: the plan and
the ontology's metrics, `reports/` (weekly, recurring and the QMR),
`projects/` status, and `memory/decision-log.md`. These prompts turn them
into a review, a plan or a board page you can defend line by line.
Financials outside marketing come in as an export.

| Use case | What it gets you | Skills it leans on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Quarterly review](quarterly-review.md) | The QMR story: what moved, why, and what changes next quarter | `/qmr`, `/weekly-report` |
| [Annual planning and OKRs](annual-planning-and-okrs.md) | Targets per metric, programs and owners, stress-tested before you commit | `/marketing-plan` |
| [Budget allocation](budget-allocation.md) | Where the next dollar goes, by program, with the risk shown | `/budget-pacing`, `/attribution-analysis` |
| [Board and exec updates](board-and-exec-updates.md) | The marketing pages of a board pack or exec update, with sources | `/qmr`, `/pipeline-report`, `/meeting-prep` |
| [Keeping strategy current](keeping-strategy-current.md) | Which strategy files are stale or contradict each other, and what to refresh first | `/context-freshness`, `/positioning-refresh` |
