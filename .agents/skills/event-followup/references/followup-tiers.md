# Follow-up tiers and sequences after an event

Condensed from the post-event section of the source above.

## Tiers by behaviour

| Tier | Who | Follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| engaged | attended, stayed for the offer, asked a question, or a target account | a personal email within 24 hours referencing their question, with a direct next step and a calendar link; written or reviewed by the person who spoke to them |
| left early | attended, dropped before the offer | the replay with a timestamp to what they missed, a softer CTA |
| no-show | registered, did not attend | "sorry we missed you" plus the replay with an honest deadline; two or three emails recover a meaningful share |
| replay registrant | registered after the event | as no-show, without the apology |
| customer | already a customer | to the CSM with the recording, never into a sales sequence |

For in-person events the same shape applies: hot (a real conversation
and an agreed next step) gets a personal note the same or next day; warm
(a conversation, no commitment) gets a personal note and one asset matched
to what they said; scan-only gets one light touch or nothing, never a cold
sequence.

## The replay decision

Time-limited (72 hours to a week) keeps the offer's urgency honest;
evergreen turns the recording into a standing lead magnet with a permanent
CTA. Pick one per event and say which in the brief.

## Closing the offer

A deadline reminder, an objection email, a final-hours note; every urgency
claim must be true.

## Recycling the content

Transcript to a recap post; audio and video to clips; numbers stated on
stage to quotable lines. Hand these to the `repurpose` workflow through the
project brief rather than doing them here.

## Diagnostics worth putting in the hand-over

Show-up rate under 30 percent points at reminders or the gap between
registration and the date; a drop before minute 40 at front-loaded
housekeeping; high hold with low conversion at the transition or the offer.
Cost per qualified attendee and per opportunity, against the team's other
channels, is what decides whether to run it again.
