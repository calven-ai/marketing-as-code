# Product marketing

Everything you ship is a synthesis: the market, the buyer, the product and
what happened in deals. In this repo that lives in `strategy/` (positioning,
messaging, ICP, personas, product brief, one battlecard per competitor),
`memory/` (decisions and call transcripts) and `data/crm/` (closed deals).
These prompts turn it into a readout, a teardown or a fact-check before
something ships. Which story to tell stays with you.

| Use case | What it gets you | Skills it leans on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Launch messaging](launch-messaging.md) | The story, the claims and the kit for a launch, built on the approved positioning | `/launch-plan`, `/messaging-house`, `/release-notes-to-marketing` |
| [Win/loss readouts](win-loss-readouts.md) | The readout on why deals were won and lost, and whether the movement is real | `/win-loss` |
| [Competitive teardowns](competitive-teardowns.md) | A full read on one competitor, from battlecard to the deals it touches | `/battlecard`, `/competitor-watch` |
| [Messaging audits](messaging-audits.md) | Pages, decks and sales assets checked against the approved story | `/review`, `/web-copy-audit` |
| [Claim checks](claim-checks.md) | Every product, pricing and competitive claim checked before it ships | `/review` |
| [Customer evidence packs](customer-evidence-packs.md) | Quotes, proof points and stories grouped by theme, for any asset | `/voice-of-customer`, `/case-study` |
