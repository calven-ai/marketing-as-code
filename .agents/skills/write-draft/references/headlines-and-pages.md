# Headlines and page structure

Condensed from the source above and rewritten for this repo. Formulas
are starting points; the voice in `brand/voice.md` decides the words.

## The "now you can" test

Put "Now you can" in front of the headline. If the result is compelling
and true, the line works. Feature-speak fails it.

## Three-beat spine

Current discomfort the reader recognises; a better state clearly
imagined; the specific step that closes the gap. A page, an email or a
post in that order rarely reads as pushy.

## Headline formulas

- Outcome: "{outcome} without {pain}", "{outcome} by {how}", "Turn
  {input} into {outcome}", "{outcome} in {timeframe}"
- Problem: "Never {unpleasant event} again", the pain as a question,
  "Stop {pain}. Start {pleasure}."
- Audience: "{product type} for {audience}", "{product type} for
  {audience} to {use}", "You don't have to {resource} to {outcome}"
- Differentiation: "The {opposite of the usual way} way to {outcome}",
  "The {category} that {differentiator}"
- Proof: "{number} {people} use {product} to {outcome}"
- Others: "The simple way to {outcome}", "Finally, {category} that
  {benefit}", "Everything you need to {outcome}", "What if you could
  {outcome}?"

Write ten, keep one. The proof formulas need a number from a snapshot.

## Page sections

Hero (headline, subheadline of one or two sentences, primary CTA,
visual), social proof bar (logos, one metric, rating), problem in the
reader's words, three to five benefits each with headline, one or two
lines and a proof, how it works in three or four verbs ("Connect, set,
get"), objections (FAQ, comparison, guarantee), final CTA with the value
restated and the risk removed.

Templates:

- Compact: hero, proof bar, three benefits, testimonial, how it works,
  final CTA.
- Full: hero, proof bar, problem, how it works, benefits, testimonial,
  use cases, comparison, case study snippet, FAQ, final CTA.
- Enterprise: hero, logo bar, business problem, solution, use cases by
  role, security and compliance, integrations, case study with numbers,
  value, demo CTA.
- Launch: announcement hero, demo video, three to five highlights,
  before and after, early testimonials, launch offer, CTA.

## CTA copy

Verb plus what they get plus a qualifier when needed: "Start my free
trial", "Get the checklist", "See pricing for my team". Avoid "Submit",
"Learn more", "Click here", "Get started".

## Perception gap

The same benefit is a selling point to one segment and a warning sign to
another. Name the segment the page serves; if two, split the page or the
traffic.

## Section writing

Problem: open with a recognisable moment, then the specific cost.
Benefits: outcome as headline, one or two sentences, a proof.
Testimonials: a specific result, before and after, role and company.
