# Pricing objections

Current truth, updated in place; the decisions behind it are in the
decision log.

- **"The status page is free with Northstar."** Heard in six of the last
  ten discovery calls. The answer that works: agree, then ask who writes
  the customer update and who reads the postmortem. We are not selling the
  page.
- **"Per-seat pricing punishes a big support team."** Heard twice from
  Tier 1 accounts. Product is evaluating a per-workspace plan; until then,
  sales caps seats at ten for support teams larger than that.
- **What does not come up:** the price itself. Nobody has said Beacon is
  too expensive; they have said it is another tool.
