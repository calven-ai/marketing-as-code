# Brand and communications

You tell the company's story more than any other team, and you get
corrected in public when it drifts. The approved story is in
`strategy/positioning.md` and `strategy/messaging.md`, the voice in
`brand/voice.md`, and what was decided and said before in `memory/` and
`content/`. These prompts keep every release, post and statement inside
those lines. Media lists and scheduling stay in your PR tools.

| Use case | What it gets you | Skills it leans on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Press releases](press-releases.md) | An announcement with checked facts, approved quotes and a pitch angle | `/press-release`, `/media-outreach` |
| [Executive thought leadership](executive-thought-leadership.md) | Posts and bylines in an executive's voice with a real point of view | `/social-post`, `/repurpose` |
| [Voice consistency audit](voice-consistency-audit.md) | Every channel's copy checked against the voice and the messaging | `/review`, `/web-copy-audit`, `/voice-refresh` |
| [Competitive response](competitive-response.md) | What you say, inside and outside, when a competitor launches, reprices or attacks | `/competitor-watch`, `/battlecard` |
