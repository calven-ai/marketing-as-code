Organic search carried the quarter: 45 percent of sessions and half of
signups. Signups missed because paid social sent visitors who did not
convert, which is also why pipeline grew faster than signups did.

## Channels

Organic 14,200 sessions and 402 signups; direct 7,900 and 188; referral
4,100 and 96; social 2,600 and 71; email 2,600 and 55. The ranking check
on 2026-06-30 has the category term at 11 and the template piece at 4.
Social's signup rate, 2.7 percent, is the lowest of any channel and was
paid.

## Projects and campaigns

The template piece was the quarter's only project and hit its goal of 80
signups. No projects closed.

## Wins, misses, lessons

- **Win:** the template piece is the best-performing page on the site,
  with one keyword and one clear next step.
- **Miss:** paid social, 2,600 sessions for 71 signups.
- **Lesson → change:** content that ranks for a problem beats ads to a
  product page. Q3 puts the launch behind a piece, not an ad.
