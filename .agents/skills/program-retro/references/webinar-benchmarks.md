# Webinar funnel: ranges, follow-up, diagnosis

## Funnel ranges (B2B, orientation only)

| Step | Range |
| --- | --- |
| visitor to registrant | 30 to 50% warm audience; 10 to 25% cold |
| registrant to live attendee | 35 to 45% |
| attendee still there after minute 40 | 50 to 70% |
| attendee to next step | 5 to 15% for a soft call to action; 1 to 5% for a direct purchase |

Compute each from the `<event>-attendees` snapshot
(`registered`, `attended`, `minutes_watched`) and the CRM's meetings or
opportunities with the event as source.

## Follow-up by segment, and timing

- Engaged attendees (stayed for the offer, asked a question): personal
  outreach within 24 hours referencing their question; a direct path to
  the next step.
- Early leavers: replay plus a timestamped link to what they missed; a
  softer call to action.
- No-shows and replay registrants: "sorry we missed you" with the replay
  and a deadline; a two-to-three email sequence recovers a meaningful
  share.
- Replay window: 72 hours to a week for a launch or offer; evergreen for
  education-led capture, where the replay becomes a standing asset.

The retro records the actual send times per segment against these.

## Diagnosis

| Symptom | Likely cause | Check first |
| --- | --- | --- |
| low registration | topic, title or promise misaligned, or traffic quality | validate the offer before adding promotion |
| show-up under 30% | reminder gaps, or too long between registration and the event | calendar-add rate at registration; the T-5-minute reminder with the join link |
| low hold | content front-loaded or the promise not kept | the drop-off timestamp in the recording |
| high hold, low conversion | the transition to the offer, or the offer itself | the audience was engaged and got no reason to act |

Calendar integration at registration is the single highest-leverage fix
for attendance; delivering the promised content is the first conversion
lever.
