# Four sequence templates

Condensed from the sequence templates above, with the timing rules and
copy guidance from the same skill's SKILL.md (MIT). Day offsets are
starting points; the team's own send history in `data/email/snapshots/`
wins.

## Welcome (after a signup to the list), 7 emails, 12 to 14 days

| Email | Day | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0 | deliver what was promised; one next action |
| 2 | 1 to 2 | a quick win they can have today |
| 3 | 3 to 4 | why we exist; the positioning as a story |
| 4 | 5 to 6 | a case study or quote as proof |
| 5 | 7 to 8 | the common objection, reframed |
| 6 | 9 to 11 | an under-used capability with its benefit |
| 7 | 12 to 14 | the ask: value recap, risk reversal |

Trigger: list signup. Exit: converted, or the sequence ends.

## Lead nurture (after a download or gated asset), 8 emails, 19 to 21 days

| Email | Day | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0 | deliver the asset; who we are in two lines |
| 2 | 2 to 3 | expand the topic with related content |
| 3 | 4 to 5 | articulate their problem better than they do |
| 4 | 6 to 8 | our method as an educational framework |
| 5 | 9 to 11 | customer results with specific numbers |
| 6 | 12 to 14 | how the approach differs from the alternatives |
| 7 | 15 to 18 | FAQ: the concerns sales hears |
| 8 | 19 to 21 | the direct offer |

Trigger: the download event. Exit: opportunity created, meeting booked,
or disengagement (no opens across four emails).

## Onboarding (after a product signup), 7 emails, 14 or more days

| Email | Day | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0 | confirm; the one first action that predicts activation |
| 2 | 1 | help if step one is not done (skip if it is) |
| 3 | 2 to 3 | the key capability, with a use case |
| 4 | 4 to 5 | a customer story they can relate to |
| 5 | 7 | check-in; ask for feedback, offer help |
| 6 | 10 to 12 | a power feature, for the engaged only |
| 7 | 14 or more | upgrade or expansion |

Trigger: the signup event in `data/ontology/events.md`. Exit: activated
or upgraded (skip to the next stage), churned.

## Re-engagement (after 30 to 60 days of inactivity), 4 emails, 10 to 14 days

1. Day 0: a genuine check-in, ask what changed.
2. Day 2 to 3: what they achieved before; what is new.
3. Day 5 to 7: a time-limited incentive, only if the team offers one.
4. Day 10 to 14: "should we stop emailing you?" with a one-click choice.

Exit: any click, or preference confirmed, or removal from the list.

## Timing and copy rules

- First email immediately; 1 to 2 days between early sends, 2 to 4 in the
  nurture phase, weekly or fortnightly after. B2B: skip weekends; send
  in the recipient's time zone.
- Subject 40 to 60 characters, clear over clever; preview text 90 to 140
  characters that extends the subject.
- Body: hook, context, value, one CTA, warm sign-off. Paragraphs of one to
  three sentences. Transactional 50 to 125 words, educational 150 to
  300, story 300 to 500.
- One email, one job. Value before the ask. Fewer, better emails over
  volume.
