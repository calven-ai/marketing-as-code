# examples/

Beacon is a fictional B2B company. `beacon/` shows what this repo's
templates look like once a team has filled them: a positioning, messaging,
an ideal customer profile, one persona, one battlecard, a voice guide, the
metric and funnel definitions, five keywords with a snapshot, a project with
its status, a published post, three logged decisions, and a quarterly review
with its dashboard. Every path mirrors the real one, so you can open the
template and the example side by side.

It is not your data. Delete this folder when you adopt the repo
(`docs/make-it-yours.md`, step 1); `python3 scripts/doctor.py` reminds you
once your own templates are filled. The dates are frozen in mid-2026, and
the check's test suite overlays this folder onto a fixture repository to
prove it passes every rule.
