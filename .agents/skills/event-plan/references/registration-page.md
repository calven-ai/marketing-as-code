# Registration page and the repurposing plan

Condensed from the source above, toned down to B2B: no fake scarcity, no
"secrets", urgency only when it is true.

## The page, top to bottom

1. A headline that promises the transformation; a subhead with the core
   benefit.
2. Date and time with a time-zone converter; one high-contrast CTA.
3. A hero image or the presenter's photo.
4. "What you will learn": three to five promises framed as outcomes, one
   core idea with several hooks.
5. Credibility: experience, the story behind the talk, recognition.
6. Urgency only if real: a countdown to the date, a live-only bonus, a
   registration deadline. Never "seats are limited" for a webinar.
7. Social proof: past attendee quotes, customer stories.

After registration: a thank-you page with calendar links; a confirmation
email with date, time zone and join link; reminders at 24 hours, 1 hour,
and "starting now".

Copy rules: conversational, benefit-led, a line break every two to four
lines, a logical close and an emotional close. In this repo, all of it in
`brand/voice.md`'s voice.

## One webinar, many assets

| Asset | Count |
| --- | --- |
| blog posts (a long recap, deep dives, an FAQ from Q&A) | 3 to 5 |
| LinkedIn posts | 3 to 5 |
| a thread | 10 to 15 posts |
| email sequence (highlights, deep dives, Q&A, resources, CTA) | 5 to 7 |
| video clips (hook, insight, demo, story, Q&A) | 3 to 5 |
| quote graphics | 5 plus |
| sales one-pager (objections with timestamps, differentiators, demo moments) | 1 |

Quick wins in about three and a half hours: the thread, the thank-you
email, three quote graphics, the recap outline, one LinkedIn post.

Distribution: day 1 the thread and the thank-you; week 1 the first post,
first clip and emails on days 1, 3, 5, 7; week 2 the second post and an
infographic; week 3 the FAQ and the clips; then monthly reuse.

## Metrics to watch

Recap traffic, per-platform reach, email opens and clicks, clip view
duration, demo requests, and whether sales actually uses the one-pager.
