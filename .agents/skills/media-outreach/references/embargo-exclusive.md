# Embargo, exclusive, or wide

Condensed from the source above.

## Decision

| Shape | Use when | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| embargo | several outlets will cover; coordinated impact wanted | the news is not strong enough for broad coverage |
| exclusive | one tier-1 outlet wants a feature-length treatment | time-sensitive news that needs wide reach |
| soft exclusive | one outlet publishes first, others follow | guaranteed coverage is essential |
| neither | moderate news; relationships matter more | major news that warrants coordination |

Exclusive favours: a complex story, a strong single relationship, major
news (funding, acquisition), a cascade of follow-on pieces. Embargo
favours: low to medium complexity, many good relationships, simultaneous
mentions, a fixed date.

## Embargo rules

- Explicit acceptance in writing before any material is shared.
- Exact lift date, time and time zone ("Tuesday 7 September, 07:00 CET").
- 5 to 15 journalists, never 50; tier 1 first, tier 2 and 3 the final
  week.
- Watermark sensitive documents; keep every acceptance on file.
- A contingency for an early break: confirm it is your story, contact the
  outlet (it may be an accident), tell everyone else, lift at once if it
  is significant.
- Never add names the day before; never widen the story mid-embargo;
  never retaliate against an outlet that could not cover.

## Timeline

Weeks 3 to 2 before: materials, the 5 to 15 names, the agreement wording.
Weeks 2 to 1: tier-1 outreach, acceptances, briefings scheduled, materials
sent to confirmed names. Final week: tier 2 and 3, briefings, questions.
Lift day: confirm timing, distribute the release, be reachable, monitor,
thank.

After: first hour available for questions; day 2 follow up with those who
did not cover; week 1 pitch secondary angles to outlets not yet covered.

## Lead times by news type

Funding 1 to 2 weeks; product launch 2 to 3; partnership 1 to 2; executive
hire 1; acquisition 1 to 3; research or a report 2 to 4.

## Note shapes

Embargo note: one line on beat relevance; the news and date; the exact
lift; three bullet points; what you can offer (data, demo, access); "no
response needed if irrelevant". Exclusive note: the news in one sentence;
what the outlet gets first; two or three unique-access bullets; a
publish-by date; the executive call on offer.
