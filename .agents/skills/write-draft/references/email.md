# Email rules

Condensed from the source above and rewritten for this repo. Sequences
are drafted here and staged elsewhere; nothing sends from a skill.

## Subject and preview

- Clear beats clever, specific beats vague; 40 to 60 characters.
- Patterns: a question ("Still struggling with X?"), how-to with a
  timeframe, a number ("3 ways to ..."), a direct personal line ("Your
  {thing} is ready"), a story tease ("The mistake I made with ...").
- Preview text of 90 to 140 characters extends the subject; it never
  repeats it.
- Emoji polarise; test, do not assume.

## Length

Transactional 50 to 125 words; educational 150 to 300; story-driven 300
to 500.

## Structure and the one-CTA rule

Hook, context, value, one CTA, a human sign-off. Paragraphs of one to
three sentences, white space, bullets where they help, little bold. One
primary action as a button; anything secondary is a plain link. Write
for the phone first.

## Sequence shapes

- Welcome, five to seven emails over 12 to 14 days: welcome and the
  promised value at once; a quick win (day 1 to 2); story and
  positioning (3 to 4); social proof (5 to 6); objections (7 to 8); the
  core feature (9 to 11); the ask (12 to 14).
- Lead nurture, six to eight emails over two to three weeks: education,
  the problem in depth, a framework, a case study, differentiation,
  objections, a direct offer.
- Re-engagement, three or four emails over two weeks, triggered at 30 to
  60 days of inactivity: check-in, value reminder, incentive, last call.
- Onboarding, five to seven emails over 14 days: activation and the aha
  moment, coordinated with what the product shows in-app rather than
  repeating it.

## Timing

Welcome immediately; early sequence one to two days apart; nurture two to
four days apart; long-term nurture weekly or every two weeks. B2B avoids
weekends; send in the recipient's time zone where the tool allows.
