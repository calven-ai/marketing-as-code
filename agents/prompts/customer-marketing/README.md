# Customer marketing

Your work runs on evidence: who said what, which account proved which
outcome, and who is ready to say it in public. That evidence lands in
`memory/transcripts/processed/`, `data/reviews/` and `data/crm/`; the story
it supports is in `strategy/`. These prompts find the right customer, the
right quote and the right moment. Usage, contracts and support history
come in as CRM or billing exports.

| Use case | What it gets you | Skills it leans on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Case studies](case-studies.md) | The account to feature, the interview, the draft and the approval checklist | `/case-study` |
| [References and reviews](references-and-reviews.md) | Who to ask for a reference or a review, for which deal, with the ask drafted | `/advocacy-program`, `/review-monitor` |
| [Customer feedback digest](customer-feedback-digest.md) | What customers said this month, by theme, and what it should change | `/voice-of-customer` |
| [Expansion campaigns](expansion-campaigns.md) | Cross-sell and upsell campaigns aimed at accounts that are actually ready | `/expansion-play`, `/churn-signals` |
| [Release communications](release-communications.md) | What changed, told to customers in the words of the persona who uses it | `/release-notes-to-marketing`, `/newsletter` |
