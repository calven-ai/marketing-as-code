## ICP Fit Scorecard

A weighted rubric for account fit. Keep fit separate from buying intent. Swap
in your own criteria; weights total 100.

| Criterion | Weight | Scoring guide |
| --- | :---: | --- |
| Industry / vertical fit | 25 | [core 25, adjacent 12, other 0] |
| Company size / revenue | 20 | [ideal 20, acceptable 12, below 0] |
| Buyer + sponsor present | 20 | [both 20, one 10, neither 0] |
| Tech-stack / operational fit | 15 | [in use 15, planned 8, none 0] |
| Active trigger | 15 | [strong 15, soft 7, none 0] |
| Complexity / scale of need | 5 | [high 5, moderate 3, low 0] |
| **Total** | **100** | **80 and above Tier 1; 50 to 79 Tier 2; below 50 Tier 3 or disqualify** |
