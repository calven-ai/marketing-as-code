# Audience segments are live

**Subject:** Tell only the customers who are affected

**Preheader:** Audience segments are live in Beacon: by region, plan and feature.

---

Hi {{first_name}},

When something breaks in one region, every customer on your status page
hears about it. The ones who were fine start opening tickets to ask
whether they are next. Half the tickets in an outage come from them.

From today, you can tell only the customers who are affected.

**What changed.** Audience segments group your customers by region, plan
or feature. When you open an incident, pick the segments it touches. The
update goes to those customers on the status page, in the product and in
the inbox. Everyone else has a quiet afternoon.

**What stays the same.** Your templates, your voice, your status page.
Segments sit on top of what you already set up.

**Why it matters.** One design-tool customer cut incident tickets by 31
percent in the quarter after they started sending updates to the
affected customers only.

**Where to start.** Settings, then Audiences. Most teams build their
first segments from the regions and plans they already keep in their
CRM.

[Set up your first segment](https://example.com/app/settings/audiences)

If you would rather see it first, reply to this email and we will walk
you through it on a call.
