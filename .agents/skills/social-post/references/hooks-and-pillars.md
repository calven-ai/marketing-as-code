# Hooks, pillars and repurposing

Condensed from coreyhaines31/marketingskills `social`: the platform
table, the pillar mix, the hook formulas and the repurposing paths a
B2B team uses.

## Platform quick reference

| Platform | Best for | Cadence | Format |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn | B2B, thought leadership | 3 to 5 a week | text posts, document posts (carousels) |
| X | tech, real time, community | several a day | threads, short takes |

The source also covers Instagram, TikTok and Facebook; not this repo's
default channels.

## Content pillars (a founder-led B2B mix)

| Pillar | Share | Topics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Industry insight | 30 percent | trends, data, predictions |
| Behind the scenes | 25 percent | building the company, lessons |
| Educational | 25 percent | how-tos, frameworks |
| Personal | 15 percent | stories, values, takes |
| Promotional | 5 percent | product updates, offers |

For each pillar ask: what unique view do we have, what does the audience
ask, what worked before, what can we sustain, what serves the goal.

## Hook formulas

- Curiosity: "I was wrong about [belief]." "The real reason [outcome]
  happens isn't what you think."
- Story: "Last week, [unexpected thing] happened." "Three years ago I
  [past]. Today, [present]."
- Value: "How to [outcome] without [pain]:" "[N] [things] that
  [outcome]:" "Stop [mistake]. Do this instead:"
- Contrarian: "Unpopular opinion: [statement]." "[Common advice] is
  wrong. Here's why:"

## Repurposing one piece

- Blog post: the key stat as a post, the framework as a list post, the
  contrarian point as a take, the steps as a document post, a thread of
  the argument.
- Webinar or talk: three quotes, the one slide that landed, the question
  the audience asked most.
- Newsletter: the top item as a stand-alone post, the aside as a story.

`repurpose` does this at scale; this skill writes one post.

## Metrics that matter (what `social-performance` reads back)

Awareness: impressions, reach, follower growth. Engagement: rate,
comments (worth more than likes), reposts, saves. Conversion: link
clicks, profile visits, direct messages, attributed leads. Weekly: top
three and bottom three posts and why, engagement and reach trend, best
times from the data.

## If it is not working

Low engagement: test new hooks, times, formats; comment more on others'
posts. Reach falling: no external links in the body, post more
consistently, engage in comments, try visual formats.

## Reverse engineering a post that worked

Hook type, structure, length, format, time, what the CTA asked, which
pillar it served. Record the pattern in the what-resonates knowledge
file, not the post.
