# The webinar funnel: stages, promotion, show-up, the live arc

Condensed from the source above. The post-event tiers live in the
`event-followup` skill.

## Four stages and their bands

| Stage | Measure | Typical B2B range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| registration | visitor to registrant | 30 to 50 percent warm, 10 to 25 cold |
| show-up | registrant to attendee | 35 to 45 percent live |
| hold | still there past minute 40 | 50 to 70 percent |
| convert | attendee to the next step | 5 to 15 percent soft CTA, 1 to 5 direct |

Use the bands to size the plan backwards from the goal; the team's own
rates come from `data/events/snapshots/`.

## Stage 0: topic and offer

A problem-aware topic, not a product topic. A title with a specific
outcome, an audience, and optionally a number or a timeframe. Decide the
offer (trial, demo, audit) before writing content. One topic, one promise,
one offer.

## Stage 1: registration page and promotion

Page: the promise in the headline, audience and outcome in the subhead,
three to five "you will learn" bullets written as outcomes, a credibility
block for the speaker, date and time with time zones and a replay note, a
short form (name, email, at most one qualifying field).

Promotion over two weeks:

| Channel | Cadence |
| --- | --- |
| email list | 3 sends: announcement, a value teaser, a day-of last call |
| social | ongoing; the host's personal posts beat the brand account |
| partners | co-host to double reach; handle shared-list consent |
| paid | retarget warm traffic only; cold-to-webinar no-shows |
| speakers' networks | pre-written posts and tracked links |

## Stage 2: show-up

A registrant with a calendar entry is a different species. Cadence:
confirmation with calendar links at once; a value reminder a day before
(a specific insight, not "reminder"); one hour before; five minutes before
with the join link (the biggest single lift); SMS where consented. Keep the
gap between registration and the date short; mid-week, late morning or
early afternoon; never Monday or Friday.

## Stage 3: the live arc

| Minutes | Beat |
| --- | --- |
| 0 to 5 | restate the promise, preview the payoff, announce the offer for the end |
| 5 to 35 | three teachable points with proof; a poll or question every 7 minutes |
| 1 | a scripted, honest bridge: the product as the implementation |
| 5 to 8 | one offer, concrete, with legitimate urgency only |
| 10 plus | Q&A seeded with two or three questions; restate the offer mid-way and at the close |

Deliver the minute-one promise around minute 35 and say so; it is what
holds the room.

## Diagnostics

| Symptom | Likely cause |
| --- | --- |
| low registration | topic, title or promise mismatch; fix before adding promotion |
| show-up under 30 percent | reminder gaps or too long a gap to the date; check calendar-add rate |
| low hold | front-loaded housekeeping or a promise mismatch; find the drop timestamp |
| high hold, low conversion | the transition or the offer |

Track cost per qualified attendee and per opportunity against the other
channels; that is the go or no-go.
